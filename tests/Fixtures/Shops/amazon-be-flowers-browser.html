Product Description

Bring your imagination to life with this original LEGO Botanicals Wildflower Bouquet building kit from the Botanicals collection. Comprising 8 different varieties of flowers, this bouquet is entirely made from cleverly combined LEGO pieces and can be customized at will. With this LEGO artificial flower bouquet, adult craft enthusiasts can spend hours building and identifying each of the flowers, inspired by cornflower, lavender, yellow poppy, green chervil, leather leaf fern, gerbera, delphinium and lupine. Thanks to the adjustable stems, you can have fun and relax while trying different combinations with LEGO flowers. Placed in a vase, they will brighten up any room in your home and will be a timeless home decor that will never fade! Part of the LEGO Botanicals collection, this buildable model includes simple building instructions and various elements made from plant-based plastic produced from sustainably sourced sugarcane. These decorative LEGO flowers are therefore an ideal gift idea for nature-loving adults. As an indication, the "delphinium" with its straight stem is over 47 cm high. Contains 939 pieces
