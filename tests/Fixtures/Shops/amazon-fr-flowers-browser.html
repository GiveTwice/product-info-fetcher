Description du produit

Bring your imagination to life with this stunning LEGO Botanicals Wildflower Bouquet building kit. Comprising 8 different flower varieties, this set is entirely made from cleverly combined LEGO pieces and can be customized at will. With this artificial flower bouquet, adult craft enthusiasts can spend hours building and identifying each of the flowers, inspired by cornflower, lavender, yellow poppy, green chervil, leather leaf fern, gerbera, delphinium and lupine. Thanks to the adjustable stems, you can have fun creating different combinations of LEGO flowers. Placed in a vase, they will brighten up any room in your home and make a timeless home decor that will never fade! Part of the LEGO Botanicals collection, this buildable model includes simple building instructions and various elements made with plant-based plastic produced from sugar cane from sources. This LEGO decorative set is therefore a wonderful gift idea for nature-loving adults. As an indication, the "delphinium", with its straight stem, is over 47 cm high. The kit contains 939 pieces.
